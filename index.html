Questions

What ages are Puddle bombs for?

Ages 3 and up, always with adult supervision. They aren't for children under 3. Never leave a child alone in the bath.

Are there toys inside?

No. There's nothing hidden inside any of our bombs. The "surprise" is your child's name on the box.

Can my child use the eucalyptus shower steamers?

No. They're for adults only. Keep them away from children, and don't use them around children under 10.

What's in them? Any nut allergens?

Full ingredient lists are below and printed on every product. Puddle and Pearl bombs contain sweet almond oil (a tree nut oil) and shea butter. If anyone in your home has a tree nut allergy, please don't use them.

When will my box ship?

Pre-orders close Friday, November 13, and every Founder Box ships by Monday, November 23. If anything changes, we'll email you right away and you can choose a full refund.

What if I don't love it?

Our Love-It Guarantee means we'll replace your box or refund you.

Ingredients

Pearl Rose & Shea Bath Bomb (net wt 5.3 oz / 150 g): Sodium Bicarbonate, Citric Acid, Butyrospermum Parkii (Shea) Butter, Prunus Amygdalus Dulcis (Sweet Almond) Oil, Fragrance, Polysorbate 80, Rosa Centifolia Flower, Hamamelis Virginiana (Witch Hazel) Water, Mica.

Puddle Bath Bomb, unscented (net wt 5.3 oz / 150 g): Sodium Bicarbonate, Citric Acid, Butyrospermum Parkii (Shea) Butter, Prunus Amygdalus Dulcis (Sweet Almond) Oil, Hamamelis Virginiana (Witch Hazel) Water.

Pearl Eucalyptus Shower Steamers (net wt 1.6 oz / 45 g each): Sodium Bicarbonate, Citric Acid, Eucalyptus Globulus Leaf Oil, Hamamelis Virginiana (Witch Hazel) Water.

Contains tree nut oil: Puddle and Pearl bath bombs contain sweet almond oil.

Safety & fine print

  • Bath bombs: for external use only. Do not eat. Avoid contact with eyes; if contact occurs, rinse with water. Stop using if irritation occurs. Tub may be slippery.
  • Puddle Bath Bombs: for ages 3+. Adult supervision required. Not for children under 3. Never leave a child unattended in the bath.
  • Pearl Eucalyptus Shower Steamers: adults only. Keep away from children. Not for use by or around children under 10. Place away from the direct water stream. Avoid eyes.
